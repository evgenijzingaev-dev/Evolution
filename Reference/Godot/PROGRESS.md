## Latest heartbeat: mating and two-parent inheritance

Added reproduction_system.gd and a heritable sexual_reproduction propensity (founders retain primitive asexual division). Organisms above the sexual threshold seek mature, fed, healthy, compatible same-species partners through the spatial grid, approach them and require contact to reproduce. Both pay energy and receive a cooldown. Offspring inherit a recombined genome followed by mutation, and record both parent IDs; generation uses the older parent's generation. ## Latest heartbeat: funded offspring reserves and one-hour evolution evidence

Offspring reserves are now transferred from parents: up to 45% of the parent's reserve, capped at half the child's inherited capacity. A sexual pair funds the same reserve equally, limited by both parents. Birth also consumes 1 energy plus 0.5 per additional cell in the child's developed body. Insufficient funding leaves all reserves untouched; only successful births register mutations, species and population counters. Founders retain their separate initialization. Existing save energy fields remain compatible; this change does not conserve or model every form of biomass/chemical energy in the ecosystem.

## Latest heartbeat: finite organic reserves and tested food dependence

Removed a second source of unbounded organic energy: killing prey previously paid a fixed immediate feeding reward and independently created a fixed-yield carcass. Each death now has a finite reserve: 75% of the victim's remaining nonnegative stored energy plus funded body formation energy. Immediate predation intake is deducted from this same budget; only the remainder becomes a carcass. Body formation energy uses the same single formula as parental birth costs (1 plus 0.5 per additional developed cell), so an unprovided body reserve is not invented on every generation. No new mutable body-mass field is needed because body structure is genetically developed and fixed for the organism.

Nutrient feeding retains its existing abstract yield. Consumed slots are set to zero reserve; mineral regrowth and slot reuse initialize the appropriate reserve.
